Where Limits End, the Future Begins

The new Paddletek Honeyfoam™ core combines three advanced foam types into a unified structure, creating a softer and more responsive feel at impact. The multi-foam design increases dwell time for improved control, expands the sweet spot for more consistent performance, and delivers long-term durability. This is a core engineered for players who demand precision, power, and reliability in every rally.

Note about 14 mm: The Honeyfoam™ TKO-X 14 mm was engineered to push performance beyond conventional limits. Approved for professional and amateur UPA competition, it exceeded the power threshold required for USAP-sanctioned events. As a result, a special “UPA Approved” sticker has been applied over the original embossed USAP mark. The performance hasn't changed—and neither has the intent behind Honeyfoam™.

Technology Overview

Engineered with a proprietary three-foam architecture, Honeyfoam™ begins with a low-density EPP core, strategically wrapped from the 4 to 8 o’clock positions in a high-performance EVA foam to enhance energy return, extend dwell time, and deliver an exponential power response. Completing the system is a perimeter foam ring designed to amplify stability, expand the sweet spot, and elevate overall forgiveness.
Featuring an advanced impact-dampening material, our edge guard actively absorbs shock, minimizes vibration transfer, and reinforces the paddle's perimeter for superior protection and durability.
This precision-engineered hitting surface generates elite-level spin and maintains its grit even under high-intensity play.
Designed for maximum reach and optimized power, the elongated geometry extends the sweet spot for improved consistency and explosive shot-making across the entire paddle face.

Bob G.

This paddle checks all the boxes. The Honeyfoam TKO-X delivers exceptional touch, impressive spin, and plenty of power when you need it, all while maintaining outstanding control. The grit grabs the ball extremely well, making it easy to shape shots, generate heavy topspin, and confidently execute resets, drops, and drives.
What stands out most to me is the feel. You can actually sense the ball making contact with the face of the paddle, and the Honeyfoam core does an excellent job absorbing shock and vibration. That combination creates a connected, responsive feel that allows the ball to dwell on the paddle just long enough to inspire confidence without feeling mushy. For players who value touch and feedback, this is a huge advantage.
Another pleasant surprise was how quickly I got dialed in with this paddle. Coming from a Selkirk BoomStik and a Joola Magnus Pro IV, I've experienced paddles that required a significant adjustment period. That wasn't the case here. From the first session, the Honeyfoam TKO-X felt natural and intuitive. The transition was seamless, and I found myself trusting it almost immediately.
For my game, the 14mm version strikes the perfect balance between control and pop. It has officially become my go-to paddle for doubles play, where touch, resets, and quick hands are critical. I still reach for the Magnus Pro IV when playing singles or skinny singles, but for doubles, the Honeyfoam TKO-X has earned a permanent spot in my bag.
The only drawback is that the paddle is currently approved by the UPA/PPA but not by USA Pickleball. For players who compete exclusively in USA Pickleball-sanctioned events, that's an important consideration. Outside of that limitation, this paddle has been a home run for me and is one of the easiest transitions I've experienced in years of testing and competing with different paddles.
Overall, this paddle offers a rare blend of touch, spin, control, and accessible power. If you're looking for a paddle that feels connected, forgiving, and tournament-ready (outside of USAP events), the Honeyfoam TKO-X 14mm is absolutely worth a serious look.
