Details

Balanced hybrid shaped paddle that leans towards poppy power thanks to a 12.7mm MachPro Polymer core, foam perimeter, Raw Toray T700 Carbon hitting surface and a 5 3/8” octagonal handle.

Middleweight/Medium Grip

Engage Pursuit Pro1 Hybrid Carbon Pickleball Paddle

Following the success of the Pursuit Pro, Engage decided to run with the well-loved tech with new shapes and core thicknesses to cater to more pickleball players than ever before. The Engage Pursuit Pro1 Hybrid Pickleball Paddle balances the reach of the original Pro1 with the forgiveness of the widebody model in a goldilocks hybrid shape right in the middle of the two. The Pursuit Pro1 Hybrid slaps when it comes to power and pop, yet it still brings big spin and enough stability for us to bend the ball where we need it to go. This balanced performance starts with the thin 12.7mm MachPro Polymer core with what Engage calls their Vortex Barrier Edge Technology. This combination of materials enhances dwell time and sweet spot size while eating up vibrations and returning an impressive amount of energy to the ball with every strike.

The 8.0-ounce Engage Pursuit Pro1 Hybrid complements its poppy core technology with a 7 3/4” wide Raw Toray T700 Carbon Fiber hitting surface. Designed to feel soft yet enhance dwell time, this naturally textured surface also brings long lasting tack for spinning shots from anywhere on the court. The Uniform Guard Technology and 5 3/8” octagonal handle completes this exceptional package by offering durability, more vibration damping, and a familiar feel.
