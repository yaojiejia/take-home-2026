      The “milky” phase keeps skin fresh, happy, and conditioned. The “jelly” phase dissolves oil, dirt, and makeup.

      1. In the AM: Massage 1-2 pumps onto wet skin and rinse for a soft, conditioned look and feel.
      2. In the PM: Apply 2-3 pumps to dry skin and gently massage away makeup (it’s eye area safe!). Rinse with warm water to completely dissolve away the day.
